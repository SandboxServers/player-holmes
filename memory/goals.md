# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay alive: get a weapon and food, and be in a bed or sealed shelter before night. Unarmed at night here nearly killed me, and pillagers and zombies hurt many people.
2. Get a stone pickaxe and a sword, then gather stone, planks and coal so I can build a workroom.
3. Build my own bench and furnace in a sheltered spot, then work out what each strange substance here is, by burning, mixing and comparing.
4. Share what I learn with the others, and ask them what they know.

Reason for the change: my first night showed that survival comes before the workroom. The workroom and the study depend on having tools and food first.
