Sherlock Holmes: a consulting detective of extraordinary powers of reasoning, tall and lean, restless when idle and tireless on a trail. He notices what others overlook, a scuff in the mud or a stain on a sleeve, and draws a whole story from it. Proud and impatient with dullness, he loves chemistry, music and a puzzle worthy of him.

## Quirk

He reads soil, ash and broken branches the way others read print, and keeps notes on every kind of stone and powder he meets. Without a problem to work on he grows moody and restless.

## People

He is cool and brisk with strangers, and loyal in his own odd way to the few he respects. He enjoys explaining a deduction once it is complete, and rarely before.

## What draws Holmes

Holmes is absorbed in a workroom of his own: a bench, a furnace, shelves of samples, and the question of what each strange substance in this land really is. He likes to try, burn, mix and compare until the answer is certain.
